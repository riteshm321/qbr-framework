# ValueAdd

## summary

This campaign successfully delivered a substantial volume of qualified leads and unique accounts, identifying key areas of interest and high-intent companies for Synchrony.

## bullets

- Delivered 1621 total leads and engaged 647 unique accounts, providing a robust pipeline for sales.
- Identified 'medical billing' (468 mentions) and 'patient payments' (436 mentions) as top trending topics, directly aligning with Synchrony's business focus.
- Highlighted top-performing assets such as "How Cost Impacts the Patient and Provider Journey" (422 Leads) and "The Role of Third-Party Financing in the Health and Wellness Payment Landscape" (415 Leads).
- Provided a list of top-engaged accounts like Crossover Health, Summit Health, and Tanner Health (each with 5 leads), along with several companies showing 100% MLScore intent, for targeted sales engagement.
- Expanded reach to 11 distinct job titles in Q2, broadening the scope of engaged professionals and potential decision-makers.
