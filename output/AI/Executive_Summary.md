# ExecutiveSummary

## short

The Synchrony CCB2B - HIA incremental 8 campaign demonstrated stable lead generation and strong growth in unique accounts this quarter.

## long

The Synchrony CCB2B - HIA incremental 8 lead generation campaign successfully delivered 1621 total leads and engaged 647 unique accounts across two quarters. Notably, Q2 saw a robust 18.58% increase in unique accounts compared to Q1, indicating expanding market penetration. Total leads maintained a stable growth trajectory with a 0.75% increase quarter-over-quarter. The consistent engagement with 4 assets and exploration of 19 trending topics, predominantly in financial and administrative categories, highlights effective content strategy.

## bullets

- Generated a total of 1621 leads for the program.
- Engaged 647 unique accounts overall, with an 18.58% increase in unique accounts quarter-over-quarter.
- Achieved a stable 0.75% growth in total leads from Q1 to Q2.
